Balancing Pitfalls

Tips on what to avoid when designing custom content.

Treat this as a strong guide rather than a set of strict rules. Taking note of these can help you better prepare balanced content before publishing.

If you're uncertain on anything you can post clips or talk about what you're working on in Discord for faster feedback. Generally this is enough / you don't need to memorize all of these tips, but this list can still help if you want to be more confident in what you're uploading.

Keep in mind you will usually need to come back and rebalance a mod after publishing at least once or twice. If anything stands out other players or mod creators will give initial impressions, but generally are open to letting mods live for a week without any changes to allow time to be proven right or wrong.

  • Using a custom tile or status without full knowledge of its intended usage
    • It's recommended to reach out to the mod author to know if your use matches their vision for how their content to should be balanced
  • Non-careful usage of Cursor / Break / Wind / PierceGuard / PierceInvis
    • These elements are difficult to use as they strip defenses and can add an extra multiplier (Freeze + Break)
  • Being unaware of whether an element is multi-hit or non-multi-hit on chips
    • Boosting wood +50 can make some scary bees
    • Looking at Link Navi System Boosts can help hint at this
  • Chips:
    • Secondary element outside of primary null or sword
      • May add tile interactions or extra multipliers
    • Not using BN6 chips for damage reference
      • Most of the content in Hub OS is scaled around BN6 N1GPedia
      • Consider your secondary effects, requirements, how hard it is to land, and whether it's * code or a strict code. Here are some suggested ranges for your base damage total:
        • Standard: 100-200
        • Mega: V1 100-200, SP 200-400
        • Giga: 300-500
        • PA: 400-600
        • Dark: 200-400
    • Copying strong unique chip abilities
      • Anything mimicking an AreaGrab is very powerful, MetaGel was nerfed to one code with the PA in a state of flux.
      • New defense chips should be in a non * code to reduce excessive availability.
  • Navis:
    • Setting playable navi health to anything other than 1000
      • This should be used at best for story telling purposes (dark navi has -3 HP from chip usage, bulky / frail navi has +-100 HP) and the adjustment should be kept low to avoid requiring heavy mechanic balancing.
      • People have tried balancing 200 HP navis in two ways heavy stalling, or powerful enough to beat opponents in the few turns they can survive. Both affect gameplay in ways that are not fun to play against.
    • Being unaware of whether an element is multi-hit or non-multi-hit on chips
      • Boosting wood +50 can make some scary bees
      • Looking at Link Navi System Boosts can help hint at this

The Greatest Pitfall: Treating criticism and feedback as negativity.

Getting pushback can be scary. Treat it as an opportunity to learn, and a place for your peers to catch you where you fall, and you'll achieve greater personal growth.
